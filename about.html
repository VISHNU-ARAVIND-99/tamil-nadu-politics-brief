About Tamil Nadu Politics Brief

Tamil Nadu Politics Brief is a free, non-partisan digital political newspaper dedicated to helping readers understand governance, political parties, legislative actions, court decisions, and district-level developments across Tamil Nadu in clear, simple English.

Two editions are published every day at 6:00 AM IST (Morning Brief) and 6:00 PM IST (Evening Brief), summarizing verified reporting from major publications and official releases so you can stay informed in 15 minutes.

Every story links directly to its original source publication for full transparency.
