1. Purpose of the Project

Tamil Nadu Politics Brief is a public-interest digital newspaper dedicated to helping citizens, researchers, students, and journalists follow political and policy developments across Tamil Nadu in plain English without spending hours monitoring dozens of television channels and newspapers.

2. Twice-Daily Coverage Windows (IST)

Two editions are generated automatically every day in the Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30) timezone:

  • Morning Edition (6:00 AM IST): Covers 6:00 PM IST of the previous day to 6:00 AM IST of the current day.
  • Evening Edition (6:00 PM IST): Covers 6:00 AM IST to 6:00 PM IST of the current day.

3. News Collection & Copyright Respect

Our automated collector reads publicly accessible RSS feeds and official government press releases configured in data/sources.json. We strictly obey robots.txt rules, never bypass paywalls, and never store or republish full copyrighted articles. Every story includes direct clickable links to the original Tamil and English publications.

4. Tamil-to-English Translation & Verification

Stories reported in Tamil publications (such as Dinamani, Dinamalar, Daily Thanthi, Hindu Tamil Thisai, Puthiya Thalaimurai, and News18 Tamil Nadu) are clustered alongside English reporting (such as The Hindu, The New Indian Express, and The News Minute) and official PIB/government releases. Significant developments are cross-checked across multiple sources, and single-source allegations are explicitly flagged.

5. Political Neutrality Charter

The system monitors DMK, AIADMK, BJP, INC, TVK, PMK, NTK, VCK, MDMK, DMDK, AMMK, Left parties, and regional movements on equal footing. It separates verified administrative actions from partisan claims and never endorses candidates or predicts election outcomes.

6. Corrections & AI Limitations Disclaimer

Summaries are synthesized automatically using AI language models constrained by programmatic link validation. While our pipeline blocks fabricated URLs and cross-checks entities, automated translations and summaries can occasionally miss nuance. Readers should always consult the linked original reporting and official Government Orders (GOs) for critical legal, financial, or policy decisions. Corrections can be submitted via the public GitHub repository's Issue tracker or triggered by administrators via GitHub Actions.
