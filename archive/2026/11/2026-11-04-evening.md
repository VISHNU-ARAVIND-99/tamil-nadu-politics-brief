## Headline: Chief Minister Vijay Unveils Rs 1,003-Crore Housing Projects as DMK Leader M.K. Stalin Alleges Power Purchase Scandals and Corporate Control

## The Big Picture
The political landscape of Tamil Nadu witnessed intense friction on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, as Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay sought to consolidate his administrative footprint while facing severe legislative and rhetorical onslaughts from the principal opposition, the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK). From the Secretariat in Chennai, Chief Minister Vijay virtually inaugurated housing complexes worth Rs 1,003 crore across five metropolitan locations, alongside pushing hard for federal decentralization through letters to Prime Minister Narendra Modi demanding a Supreme Court Bench in Chennai. However, administrative achievements were quickly overshadowed by bitter political sparring. DMK President M.K. Stalin launched a blistering attack during a felicitation event in Coimbatore, accusing the ruling Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) administration of orchestrating artificial power cuts to justify high-cost electricity procurement, describing the government as a 'circus' dictated by corporate interests.

Simultaneously, the administration found itself embroiled in high-stakes legislative survival debates. TVK MLA Mustafa alleged a massive Rs 30-crore horse-trading conspiracy by the DMK and AIADMK to topple the minority regime, drawing sharp counter-condemnations from AMMK General Secretary T.T.V. Dhinakaran and opposition party leaders. Parallel developments on the judicial and policy fronts—including the Supreme Court staying the Madras High Court order on the Chidambaram Natarajar Temple administration, the rollout of tenders for the flagship Annan Seer gold and silk saree distribution scheme, and Chief Minister Vijay reiterating state opposition to NEET before the Nandan Nilekani task force—highlighted a deeply polarized governance climate where populist welfare initiatives run parallel to volatile legislative maneuvers and intense Centre-State negotiations.

## Government and Administration
- **Tamil Nadu Government Floats Tenders for Annan Seer Scheme to Procure Gold and Silk Sarees:** Fulfilling a prominent electoral promise made during the 2026 assembly elections, the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) government led by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay officially initiated tender processes for the procurement of gold and silk sarees under the flagship 'Annan Seer' project. The scheme, designed to provide eight grams of gold and a quality silk saree for marriages from the state treasury, received an initial financial allocation of Rs 812 crores during the first budget presentation delivered by Finance Minister Maria Wilson. Administrative departments have published detailed technical specifications and quality benchmarks for procurement to ensure absolute transparency and prevent corruption in supply chains. Opposition parties, however, have questioned the fiscal sustainability of continuous welfare rollouts against the backdrop of rising state borrowings and revenue deficits. ([News18 Tamil Nadu](https://tamil.news18.com/tamil-nadu/annan-seer-scheme-tender-for-gold-and-silk-saree-procurement-nw-azt-ws-bl-2246019.html))

## Assembly, Courts, and Policy
- **CM Vijay Urges Prime Minister Modi to Establish Supreme Court Bench in Chennai:** Intensifying federal advocacy, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay addressed a formal letter to Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Wednesday, formally urging the central government to initiate immediate administrative and legislative steps for establishing a Bench of the Supreme Court of India in Chennai. Highlighting that a southern bench of the apex court has been a long-pending constitutional and legal demand of the state, Chief Minister Vijay emphasized that litigants from southern states face severe financial and logistical burdens traveling to New Delhi for final appellate hearings. Legal experts and bar association representatives have welcomed the Chief Minister's direct intervention, pointing out that regional decentralization of the highest judiciary is vital for ensuring affordable and accessible justice for citizens across peninsular India. ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-cm-vijay-urges-pm-modi-to-take-steps-to-set-up-supreme-court-bench-in-chennai/article71664956.ece))
