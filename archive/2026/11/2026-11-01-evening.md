## Top Political Developments
### 1. President, Prime Minister, and CM Vijay greet people on Tamil Nadu Day
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
President Droupadi Murmu, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar, and Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay greeted citizens on Tamil Nadu Day, acknowledging the state's growth and cultural identity. While the ruling TVK government celebrated the occasion on November 1, the opposition DMK reiterated its stance that July 18 is the historically correct date for the observance.

### 2. Tamil Nadu government orders setting up of 30 additional special courts
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
The Tamil Nadu government has ordered the creation of 30 additional special courts to fast-track trials involving sexual offences against children and drug-related crimes. Out of the newly approved courts, 26 will exclusively handle Pocso Act cases, while four will manage cases registered under the NDPS Act. The directive was issued by Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay to reduce judicial pendency and ensure timely justice.

## Government and Administration
- **TN to introduce weekly traditional attire day in government offices; CM Vijay to head Secretariat Tamil Sangam:** The Tamil Nadu government will introduce a weekly traditional attire day requiring officials to wear traditional clothing to promote local culture and support handloom sectors. Minister Rajmohan announced that Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay approved the initiative, which includes establishing a Secretariat Tamil Sangam. ## Political Parties and Opposition
- **Offered Rs 5 crore bribe for Manram chief post, alleges TVK Minister Rajmohan:** School Education Minister Rajmohan claimed he was offered a Rs 5 crore bribe to secure the chairmanship of the Iyal Isai Nataka Manram through a corporation chairman. The opposition DMK and BJP immediately criticized the minister for failing to report the bribery attempt directly to the Directorate of Vigilance and Anti-Corruption. ## Assembly, Courts, and Policy
- **Tasmac staff go on flash strike against bottle buy-back scheme, 3 suspended:** Tasmac liquor store employees launched a flash strike across Chennai and other districts to protest the empty-bottle buy-back scheme introduced to reduce environmental littering. With unions threatening continued protests due to lack of talks, the state government took a hard stance by suspending three protesting workers.
