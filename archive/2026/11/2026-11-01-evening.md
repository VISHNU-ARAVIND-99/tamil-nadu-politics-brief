### 6. Seven Rameswaram fishermen detained by Sri Lankan Navy near Katchatheevu islet
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
RAMESWARAM In a fresh incident, seven fishermen from Rameswaram and Thangachimadam in the Ramanathapuram district were arrested on charges of poaching in international waters by Sri Lankan Navy personnel in the early hours of Sunday (November 1, 2026). According to Tamil Nadu Fisheries Department officials, Sri Lankan Navy personnel apprehended seven fishermen who were fishing between the Katchatheevu islet and Mannar North at around 1 a.m. on Sunday (November 1, 2026). They were taken, along with the trawler, to a naval port in the island nation.

### 7. Five persons arrested for circulating counterfeit currencies in Vellore
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
VELLORE Five persons were arrested by Gudiyatham Town police in Vellore on Sunday for circulating counterfeit currencies of ₹500 denominations in the area. Police identified the arrested persons as Mohammed Rafiq, 48, Ahmed, 56, Kamaraj, 32, Gopal, 51, and Babu, 59. They are residents of Gudiyatham and Ambur towns. Initial inquiry revealed that the key suspect Rafiq was running a hotel in Karanampettai near Gudiyatham town. He had ordered grocery items through online delivery services.

### 8. Woman with disability dies in midnight fire at Korattur apartment
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
CHENNAI A 48-year-old woman with a disability died after being trapped in a fire that broke out at her apartment in Periyar Nagar, Korattur, in the early hours of Sunday (November 1, 2026). Her husband and son escaped with burn injuries. The deceased has been identified as Akila. The police said the incident occurred when the family of three was asleep. According to preliminary information, an air-conditioner and a refrigerator, among other electrical appliances, reportedly exploded as the fire spread rapidly through the house.
