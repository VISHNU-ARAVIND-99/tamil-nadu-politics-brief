### 1. Postponement of college exam for CM’s visit in Pudukottai triggers row
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
PUDUKOTTAI With Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay scheduled to visit Pudukottai on November 2, to inaugurate the new bus stand and distribute welfare measure, semester examinations scheduled for the day at the Kalaignar Karunanidhi Government Arts College for Women, an autonomous institution in the town, has been postponed, triggering a row. The examinations in the college started on October 30 and are to conclude on November 19.

### 2. Tamil Nadu to set up 26 POCSO Act courts and four NDPS courts
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
Chennai Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has ordered the establishment of 26 courts under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, and four courts under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985, to expedite the trial of cases registered under these laws. A State government press release issued on Sunday (November 1, 2026) said during a review meeting on cases registered under the POCSO Act, the Chief Minister had stressed the need to ensure justice for victims by speeding up trials.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-to-set-up-26-pocso-act-courts-and-four-ndps-courts/article71652310.ece)

### 4. 1,172 candidates receive offer letters at job fair held in Villupuram
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
VILLUPURAM Minister for Social Justice Vanni Arasu distributed appointment orders to 1,172 job seekers at a private sector job fair held at St. Ann’s College of Arts and Science in Tindivanam on Saturday (October 31, 2026). The event was organised jointly by the Villupuram district administration, the District Employment and Career Guidance Centre, and the Tamil Nadu State Rural Urban Livelihoods Mission, under the chairmanship of District Collector Sheik Abdul Rahman. According to a press release, more than 167 companies interviewed 4,214 candidates at the fair.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/1172-candidates-receive-offer-letters-at-job-fair-held-in-villupuram/article71652132.ece)

### 5. Seven Rameswaram fishermen detained by Sri Lankan Navy near Katchatheevu islet
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
RAMESWARAM In a fresh incident, seven fishermen from Rameswaram and Thangachimadam in the Ramanathapuram district were arrested on charges of poaching in international waters by Sri Lankan Navy personnel in the early hours of Sunday (November 1, 2026). According to Tamil Nadu Fisheries Department officials, Sri Lankan Navy personnel apprehended seven fishermen who were fishing between the Katchatheevu islet and Mannar North at around 1 a.m. on Sunday (November 1, 2026). They were taken, along with the trawler, to a naval port in the island nation.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/seven-rameswaram-fishermen-detained-by-sri-lankan-navy-near-katchatheevu-islet/article71652111.ece)

### 7. Woman with disability dies in midnight fire at Korattur apartment
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
CHENNAI A 48-year-old woman with a disability died after being trapped in a fire that broke out at her apartment in Periyar Nagar, Korattur, in the early hours of Sunday (November 1, 2026). Her husband and son escaped with burn injuries. The deceased has been identified as Akila, 48. Police said the incident occurred while the family of three was asleep. An air-conditioner and a refrigerator, among other electrical appliances, reportedly exploded as the fire spread rapidly through the house.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/woman-with-disability-dies-in-midnight-fire-at-korattur-apartment/article71652193.ece)

### 8. Minister inaugurates modern paddy storage facilities in Cuddalore district
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
CUDDALORE Tamil Nadu Minister for Food and Civil Supplies P. Venkataramanan on Saturday (October 31, 2026) inaugurated two paddy storage facilities, with structural roofing built at a total cost of ₹11.56 crore in Srimushnam taluk in Cuddalore district. The facilities, constructed with financial assistance under the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), have a combined storage capacity of 7,000 tonnes. The Melapalayur facility, with a capacity of 2,000 tonnes, was built at a cost of ₹3.83 crore, while the 5,000-tonne facility at Thethampattu cost ₹7.73 crore.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/minister-inaugurates-modern-paddy-storage-facilities-in-cuddalore-district/article71652117.ece)
