## Top Political Developments
### 1. Tamil Nadu Day Celebrated Amid Political Row Over Date
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
The Tamil Nadu government celebrated Tamil Nadu Day on November 1, with Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar and Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay extending public greetings. However, the opposition DMK objected to the date, reiterating that July 18 is the historically accurate and correct day for the celebration. The political disagreement highlights ongoing friction between the ruling party and the opposition over state heritage dates.
- **Why it matters:** Reflects the ongoing narrative battle between political parties regarding Tamil Nadu's official historical identity.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-day-vijay-government-celebrates-november-1-dmk-says-july-18-is-the-right-date/article71653101.ece)

### 2. Postponement of College Exams for Chief Minister's Visit Triggers Controversy
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
Semester examinations at Kalaignar Karunanidhi Government Arts College for Women in Pudukottai were postponed for Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay's visit on November 2. The CM is scheduled to inaugurate a new bus stand and distribute welfare measures during his maiden official visit to the district. The decision to reschedule exams to accommodate the political event sparked immediate backlash and a political row.
- **Why it matters:** Draws criticism regarding administrative priorities and the disruption of academic schedules for VIP political visits.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/postponement-of-college-exam-for-cms-visit-in-pudukottai-triggers-row/article71649447.ece)

### 3. VCK Functionary Hacked to Death in Thoothukudi, Causing Local Tension
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
A 40-year-old VCK functionary named Balamurugan was hacked to death by an unidentified gang at a petrol bunk in Thoothukudi on Sunday morning. The victim, who operated a vegetable shop and worked as a van driver, was attacked while refueling his two-wheeler. The brutal murder triggered sudden protests and widespread tension across the local area, prompting heavy police deployment to maintain law and order.
- **Why it matters:** Highlights safety concerns and political violence targeting grassroots party functionaries in the state.
