## Top Political Developments
### 1. Tamil Nadu Celebrates Formation Day
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
Tamil Nadu celebrated Tamil Nadu Day on Sunday with Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar and Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay leading the state-level events. President Droupadi Murmu, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and Chief Minister Vijay greeted the public, emphasizing the state's historical identity and national contribution. Meanwhile, the opposition DMK reiterated its stance that July 18 is the historically correct date for the celebration, creating a political debate over the official observance.

### 2. TN Government Announces Traditional Attire Day and Secretariat Tamil Sangam
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
School Education and Tamil Development Minister Rajmohan announced that Tamil Nadu will introduce a weekly traditional attire day in government offices. Ministers and employees must wear traditional clothing like a veshti or saree on a designated day to promote Tamil culture and support handloom weavers. Additionally, the government announced the establishment of a Secretariat Tamil Sangam to be headed by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay.
- **Sources:** [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/tn-to-introduce-weekly-traditional-attire-day-in-government-offices-cm-vijay-to-head-secretariat-tamil-sangam)

### 3. Chief Minister Vijay Orders Setup of 26 POCSO and Four NDPS Courts
- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has ordered the immediate establishment of 26 new courts under the POCSO Act and four courts under the NDPS Act. The government stated that the move aims to expedite pending trials and ensure swift justice for victims. The decision follows a high-level review meeting where the Chief Minister stressed the urgent need to speed up legal proceedings for sensitive cases across the state.
- **Sources:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-to-set-up-26-pocso-act-courts-and-four-ndps-courts/article71652310.ece)
