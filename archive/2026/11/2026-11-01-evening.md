### 1. Tamil Nadu to set up 26 POCSO Act courts and four NDPS courts
- **Verification Status:** Multi-Source Verified
Chennai Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has ordered the establishment of 26 courts under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, and four courts under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985, to expedite the trial of cases registered under these laws. A State government press release issued on Sunday (November 1, 2026) said during a review meeting on cases registered under the POCSO Act, the Chief Minister had stressed the need to ensure justice for victims by speeding up trials.

- **Verification Status:** Single-Source Report
RAMESWARAM In a fresh incident, seven fishermen from Rameswaram and Thangachimadam in the Ramanathapuram district were arrested on charges of poaching in international waters by Sri Lankan Navy personnel in the early hours of Sunday (November 1, 2026). According to Tamil Nadu Fisheries Department officials, Sri Lankan Navy personnel apprehended seven fishermen who were fishing between the Katchatheevu islet and Mannar North at around 1 a.m. on Sunday (November 1, 2026). They were taken, along with the trawler, to a naval port in the island nation.
