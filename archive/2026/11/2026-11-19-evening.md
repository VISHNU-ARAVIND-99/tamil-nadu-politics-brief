## Headline: Kolathur TVK MLA V.S. Babu Passes Away at 75, Sparking State-Wide Mourning and Political Tributes

## The Big Picture
Tamil Nadu's political landscape has been profoundly impacted by the sudden demise of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) MLA V.S. Babu, who famously defeated DMK president M.K. Stalin in the Kolathur constituency during the 2026 assembly elections. Passing away at the age of 75 following a prolonged respiratory illness and cardiac arrest at MGM Hospital in Aminjikarai, Chennai, Babu's death has prompted the ruling TVK government to announce a seven-day state-wide mourning period, halting all official engagements and party functions with the party flag flying at half-mast.

Simultaneously, the administration faces intense political heat from multiple fronts. Opposition leaders, including AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami, DMK president M.K. Stalin, and BJP state chief Nainar Nagenthran, have launched scathing attacks against Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay's administration. Criticisms span from a major 70 kg heroin seizure worth ₹700 crore executed by the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) in Madhavaram, Chennai, to alleged administrative failures halting the monthly ₹1,000 disbursement for lakhs of beneficiaries under the Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai scheme. Meanwhile, legislative debates over inter-state water disputes, notably the Mekedatu dam project, have intensified cross-party friction, testing the nascent governance model of Chief Minister Vijay's administration.

## Government and Administration
- **DRI Heroin Seizure Sparks Severe Opposition Backlash Over Intelligence Failures:** AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami, AMMK leader TTV Dhinakaran, and DMK president M.K. Stalin severely castigated the TVK government following a major narcotics bust where the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) seized 70 kg of heroin worth approximately ₹700 crore in Madhavaram, Chennai. Palaniswami pointed out the irony that this massive haul occurred within 24 hours of the Director General of Police holding a press conference asserting the state's strict stance against drug trafficking, questioning the functional competence of the State's Anti-Drug Intelligence Unit. Stalin echoed these concerns, alleging that Tamil Nadu has transformed from an investor-friendly destination into a safe haven for drug peddlers under the current dispensation. In response to mounting governance pressures, Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay attempted to steer policy focus toward industrial development by hosting Renault Group CEO François Provost at the Secretariat in Chennai to review manufacturing expansions at the Oragadam SIPCOT park, alongside inaugurating 12 public infrastructure projects valued at ₹175 crore. ## Political Parties and Opposition
- **BJP Accuses TVK Regime of Halting Women's Welfare Aid as Congress Urges Unity on Cauvery:** Tamil Nadu BJP president Nainar Nagenthran launched a sharp critique against the TVK government, alleging a massive betrayal of women beneficiaries under the flagship Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai scheme. Nagenthran claimed that over 50% of the 16.94 lakh beneficiaries added during the second phase in December 2025 have not received their monthly ₹1,000 financial assistance for the past three months, threatening livelihoods that previously supported 1.31 crore women statewide. Simultaneously, inter-party tensions flared over the Cauvery river dispute as Tamil Nadu Congress Committee (TNCC) president B. Manickam Tagore appealed to all regional parties to set aside mutual recriminations and unite against Karnataka's proposed Mekedatu dam project. Tagore defended Chief Minister Vijay's ongoing legal maneuvers against the dam, countering claims of executive inaction while warning that the project would turn the Cauvery delta into a drought-prone wasteland. In separate legal developments, TVK functionary Aadhav Arjuna served a formal legal notice to DMK legislator I. Paranthamen, demanding a public retraction, an unconditional apology, or ₹1 crore in damages over alleged defamatory remarks made during a television debate. ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tn-bjp-chief-nainar-nagenthran-slams-tvk-govt-over-alleged-halting-of-1000-assistance-to-women/article71719838.ece))

Joseph Vijay. Legal analysts and civil liberties advocates heavily criticized the prolonged incarceration for an offense categorized as bailable under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, raising questions about police overreach and arbitrary enforcement by local law enforcement agencies.
