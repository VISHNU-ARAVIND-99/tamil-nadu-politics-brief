## Headline: TVK Government Faces Legal and Political Turmoil Over MLA Harassment Case and Religious Row

## The Big Picture
The Tamil Nadu political landscape is currently dominated by two major crises challenging the administration of Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay. The ruling TVK party is grappling with a severe sexual harassment scandal involving its Kallakurichi MLA, Arul Vignesh, who is accused of sending inappropriate messages to a woman IAS officer. This has triggered intense pressure from the DMK and AIADMK, both of which are demanding a CBI probe and the MLA's immediate arrest, while the Madras High Court has intervened to protect the victim's identity and mandate strict workplace safeguards.

Simultaneously, the government is embroiled in a religious controversy surrounding the Karthigai Deepam festival at Tirupparankundram. Despite a Supreme Court interim order, the administration's decision to light the lamp at a traditional mandapam rather than the disputed 'deepathoon' has drawn sharp criticism from the BJP and Hindu outfits. While the government maintains it acted to preserve law and order, the opposition has labeled the move as a violation of judicial directives and an act of vote-bank politics. These dual pressures, coupled with a massive investment push through the VETTRI conclave, define a volatile period for the state's governance.
