## Headline: Political Tensions Escalate as TVK Government Faces Allegations of Instability and External Influence

## The Big Picture
The political landscape in Tamil Nadu is currently defined by intense friction between the ruling Tamizhaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) and the opposition, primarily the DMK and AIADMK. Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay’s administration is navigating a period of heightened scrutiny, with opposition leaders alleging that the government is being influenced by corporate interests and astrological considerations rather than sound policy. These claims have sparked a broader debate regarding the stability of the current legislative majority.

Simultaneously, the administration is grappling with administrative challenges, including infrastructure projects, public service delivery, and legal battles over social media regulation. As the government attempts to consolidate its position through new office setups and policy initiatives, the opposition is leveraging local grievances and alleged financial irregularities to challenge the ruling party's mandate. The state remains in a state of high political volatility as both sides prepare for upcoming legislative and public discourse.

## Government and Administration
- **Headline: CM Vijay Establishes New Camp Office at 'Vettri Illam' Amidst Administrative Shifts:** Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has officially shifted his camp office to the renovated bungalow at Raja Annamalaipuram, now renamed 'Vettri Illam'. The move, intended to streamline official functions and reduce the logistical burden on his previous Foreshore Estate residence, saw the CM working from the new facility for three hours on Thursday. Meanwhile, the administration faces criticism from the DMK, with M.K. Stalin alleging that the government is being run by corporate power centers and astrologers rather than elected officials. Additionally, Textiles Minister M. Vijay Balaji confirmed that the government will exclusively procure dhotis and sarees for Pongal distribution from state cooperative societies, doubling the order volume to support local weavers. The Chennai Corporation also took action to remove banners welcoming the CM and Jaganmohan Reddy, citing traffic obstruction, while the PMK has alleged a 'massive scam' regarding development projects in the Pallikaranai marshland. ## Assembly, Courts, and Policy
- **Headline: Legal Challenges and Policy Demands Shape Judicial Discourse:** The Madras High Court has been petitioned via a Public Interest Litigation (PIL) by lawyer Syed Aleem, seeking the establishment of clear guidelines for the police regarding the registration of cases against social media users. The petitioner argues that criminal laws are being misused to silence political critics and activists who express opinions against the government, with police allegedly registering cases mechanically without proper investigation. Simultaneously, Chief Minister Vijay has formally written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi, advocating for the establishment of a regional bench of the Supreme Court in Chennai to improve judicial access for the state's citizens. Furthermore, the government has reiterated its firm stance against the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test (NEET), maintaining that the abolition of the exam remains a top priority for the administration. These legal and policy maneuvers underscore the government's attempt to assert its autonomy while facing mounting pressure from both the judiciary and the central government. ([News18 Tamil Nadu](https://tamil.news18.com/tamil-nadu/social-media-post-case-public-interest-petition-in-high-court-nw-lks-ws-bl-2246824.html))
