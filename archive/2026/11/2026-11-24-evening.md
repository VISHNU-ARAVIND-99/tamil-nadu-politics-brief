## Headline: Madras High Court Orders IG-Level Probe as FIR is Filed Against TVK MLA Over Alleged Harassment of Kallakurichi District Collector

## The Big Picture
Tamil Nadu's political landscape faces an unprecedented governance crisis following serious allegations of misconduct involving a ruling Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) legislator and a senior woman IAS officer serving as the Kallakurichi District Collector. The controversy has ignited a fierce multi-party political confrontation, with major opposition outfits including the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) and the All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK) fiercely condemning Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay for alleged administrative inaction and protection of the accused lawmaker. 

Concurrently, religious and social tensions escalated sharply in Madurai district, where widespread protests erupted over the execution of Supreme Court directives concerning the Karthigai Maha Deepam festival at Thirupparankundram. State authorities drew severe censure from the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) for lighting the ceremonial lamp at a customary alternative site rather than the disputed 'deepathoon' pillar, culminating in the preventive detention of over 300 demonstrators, including prominent party leaders. These compounding crises place immense judicial and political scrutiny on the state administration's operational integrity and law enforcement framework.

## Government and Administration
- **Opposition Leaders Stalin and Palaniswami Target Chief Minister Vijay Over Kallakurichi Row, Demanding CBI Probe and Action Against Shielding Officials:** Major political opposition figures launched blistering attacks against Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay, accusing his administration of attempting to shelter TVK MLA C. Arul Vignesh after allegations surfaced that he harassed the Kallakurichi District Collector. DMK deputy general secretary Kanimozhi Karunanidhi publicly questioned the Chief Minister's silence on the matter, praising the District Collector for her courage while condemning the delayed police action and demanding accountability from the Chief Secretary regarding administrative reporting protocols. Simultaneously, AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami (EPS) escalated the political pressure by formally demanding that the criminal investigation be transferred immediately to the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), asserting that ruling party forces attempted to cover up the scandal. The coordinated opposition offensive underscores profound institutional concerns regarding administrative oversight, the safety of women civil servants, and governance transparency under the current regime. ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/stalin-urges-tamil-nadu-government-to-act-against-kallakurichi-tvk-mla-for-messages-to-collector/article71737120.ece))

## Political Parties and Opposition
- **BJP Leaders Detained Amid Statewide Protests Over Thirupparankundram Deepam Controversy and Alleged Defiance of Supreme Court Directives:** High-intensity political drama unfolded across Madurai district on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, as police detained approximately 300 protesters, including prominent BJP leaders Pon. Radhakrishnan, Rama Srinivasan, and state president Nainar Nagenthran, following protests against the district administration. The demonstrations were triggered after authorities lit the Karthigai Maha Deepam at the customary Deepa Mandapam near the Uchipillaiyar Temple instead of the newly identified 'deepathoon' pillar on Thirupparankundram hill, which protestors and Hindu outfits claimed violated the spirit of Supreme Court orders. BJP Tamil Nadu president Nainar Nagenthran vehemently accused state officials of deliberately deceiving the judiciary, comparing their actions to 'children being deceived with chocolates.' The ruling TVK government defended its administrative measures as necessary for maintaining public order and managing local religious sensitivities, but right-wing outfits vowed to escalate statewide agitations over the implementation of the judicial directives. ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Madurai/bjp-leaders-among-300-arrested-in-thirupparankundram/article71739565.ece))

A 26-year-old local resident was swiftly apprehended and detained by the Erode district police after he allegedly defaced life-size bronze statues of late Chief Minister M.
