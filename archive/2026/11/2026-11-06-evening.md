## Headline: Political Turmoil Escalates in Tamil Nadu: Corruption Charges, High-Profile Arrests, and Legislative Realignments Rock State Politics

## The Big Picture
The political landscape of Tamil Nadu is witnessing intense turbulence, characterized by high-stakes legal battles, corruption charges against top opposition leaders, and fierce public spats over cultural and administrative infrastructure. The ruling Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) administration, led by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay, finds itself embroiled in fierce exchanges with the principal opposition Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) over alleged financial irregularities in public projects, most notably the newly inaugurated Periyar Arivulagam library in Coimbatore. Concurrently, investigative agencies have intensified scrutiny on former DMK ministers, registering significant criminal cases involving massive financial discrepancies in sand mining and school education procurement.

Adding to the administrative friction, legislative and judicial fronts are heating up. The Supreme Court has agreed to hear petitions challenging narrow electoral outcomes, while police in Madurai and Chennai have arrested individuals linked to alleged horse-trading attempts aimed at destabilizing the state government during potential floor tests. Furthermore, strategic administrative maneuvers—such as the disbandment of the high-level committee on Union-State relations—signal a major shift in the state's policy framework, keeping citizens, analysts, and policymakers on high alert as traditional party lines undergo rapid recalibration.

## Government and Administration
- **Tamil Nadu Government Disbands High-Level Committee on Union-State Relations and Transfers Mandate to State Law Commission:** In a major administrative restructuring, the Tamil Nadu government issued a formal Government Order (G.O.) on November 6, 2026, disbanding the High-Level Committee on Union–State Relations originally constituted during the previous DMK regime in April 2025. The panel, which was tasked with reviewing constitutional provisions, federal statutes, and recommending policy measures to strengthen state autonomy, has had its entire mandate transferred to the State Law Commission. Policymakers and constitutional experts have expressed varied opinions on this administrative pivot, noting that the shift aims to streamline legal research under an established statutory body rather than a temporary political panel. The decision reflects the current TVK administration's recalibration of federal advocacy mechanisms, ensuring that structural demands for state autonomy are pursued through rigorous academic and legislative vetting by the Law Commission. ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tn-govt-disbands-high-level-committee-on-union-state-relations/article71675771.ece))

## Political Parties and Opposition
- **Political Firestorm Erupts Over Alleged Substandard Book Procurement at Coimbatore Periyar Arivulagam:** Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay and prominent literary figures have launched scathing attacks against the erstwhile DMK government following revelations of massive procurement irregularities at the newly opened Periyar Arivulagam library in Coimbatore. Renowned writer Perumal Murugan publicly flagged that thousands of substandard books, featuring suspected AI-generated texts and phantom authors, were procured using public funds amounting to crores of rupees. While CM Vijay asserted that the people of Tamil Nadu will never forgive the DMK for turning cultural institutions into corruption hubs, opposition leaders including TNCC President Manickam Tagore and BJP leader K. Annamalai have demanded an independent judicial probe and the immediate withdrawal of these publications. The DMK has fiercely denied the allegations, countering that the questionable inventory was deliberately manipulated and backdated under the current regime to tarnish the legacy of Dravidian icons. ## Assembly, Courts, and Policy
- **Madurai and Chennai Police Arrest Four Individuals in Separate Alleged Horse-Trading Conspiracies Targeting TVK MLAs:** Political tensions reached a boiling point on Friday, November 6, 2026, as police forces in Madurai and Chennai executed high-profile arrests in connection with alleged bribery and horse-trading attempts. Former Madurai Corporation Deputy Mayor P.M. Mannan, now aligned with the AIADMK, was arrested by the Madurai City Police Central Crime Branch following a formal complaint by TVK MLA V.M.S. alleging financial inducement to topple the state government. Simultaneously, Triplicane Police in Chennai arrested three men based on a complaint filed by TVK Uthangarai MLA N. Elaiyaraja for attempts to bribe him ahead of a potential no-confidence motion against the Assembly Speaker. While Mr. Mannan was subsequently released on bail, opposition parties such as the BJP have vehemently rejected the government's charges as baseless political theater designed to cover up administrative failures in the energy and law-and-order sectors. - **Judicial Custody Extended for Former DMK Minister Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi Until November 18 in School Bribery Case:** The Special Court for cases under the Prevention of Corruption Act in Chennai extended the judicial custody of former School Education Minister Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi until November 18, 2026. Arrested on October 26 by the DVAC and currently lodged in Puzhal Central Prison, Mr. Poyyamozhi faces grave charges of extortion and systemic bribery involving over Rs 100 crore collected from private educational institutions across various districts. Defense counsels argued for bail citing medical grounds and lack of active flight risk, but public prosecutors successfully demonstrated that ongoing digital forensic investigations require uninterrupted custody to prevent witness tampering. The case has sent shockwaves through district educational administrative machinery, prompting the current government to initiate statewide compliance audits of private school recognitions and fee regulatory committees to root out institutionalized corruption inherited from the previous administration.
