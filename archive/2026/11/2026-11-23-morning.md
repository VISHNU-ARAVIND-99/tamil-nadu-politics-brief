## Headline: Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay Convenes First Alliance Legislative Meet Amid Rising Tensions with DMK and AIADMK

## The Big Picture
Tamil Nadu's political landscape witnessed intense friction over the weekend as Chief Minister and TVK president C. Joseph Vijay scheduled his inaugural collective interaction with alliance and supporting party legislators at Pamani Illam on Greenways Road, Chennai. This high-stakes diplomatic and administrative outreach includes representatives from the VCK, Congress, and IUML, designed to shore up legislative stability and ensure direct channels of communication following internal friction.

Concurrently, the political arena has been rocked by fierce verbal exchanges between the ruling TVK and the principal opposition DMK, with DMK president M.K. Stalin launching a scathing attack calling the six-month-old government a 'social scourge' allegedly guided by superstition and astrologers rather than Periyar-Ambedkar ideals. The ruling dispensation forcefully retaliated through Cabinet ministers, labelling the DMK's attacks as symptomatic of a 'fascist mindset' and pointing to historical electoral malpractices as the real societal threat. Meanwhile, AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami escalated pressure on governance issues, targeting the administration over the Supreme Court's dismissal of the State's Cauvery and Mekedatu petitions, even as internal fractures within the AIADMK widened following public spats with senior leader S.P. Velumani.

The legal battle, which has wound its way through multiple judicial tiers, centers on allegations of unauthorized land occupation and subsequent institutional expansion by the educational trust. A bench of apex court justices reviewed the conflicting claims and restrained state revenue authorities from executing eviction orders or sealing disputed structures pending further hearings. Legal counsels for the university argued that abrupt state action would severely disrupt ongoing academic sessions for thousands of enrolled students. The interim stay provides significant breathing room for the institution while judicial scrutiny of land titles continues. ## District-Level Developments
- **Madras High Court Directs Ramanathapuram Collector to Probe Village Land Grabbing and Privacy Violations:** The Madurai Bench of the Madras High Court directed the Ramanathapuram district collector to initiate a comprehensive inquiry into a severe land-grabbing complaint in a local village. A division bench comprising Justice P. Velumurugan and Justice K.K. Ramakrishnan expressed deep shock after a petitioner alleged that an individual named G. Thinagaran Thevar had illegally encroached upon vast tracts of community land. The petition further highlighted that closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras installed on the encroached property violated local women's privacy and restricted access to natural needs. Counsel A. Neelmegam submitted that ruling TVK functionaries and corrupt revenue officials colluded to forge land ownership documents. The court has demanded a detailed compliance report from the district administration within a stipulated timeframe.
