## Headline: CM Vijay Issues Stern Warning to TVK Legislators Amid Misconduct Allegations; Madras HC Orders FIR in AI-Generated Book Scam

## The Big Picture
The Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) administration, led by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay, is navigating a complex political landscape as it completes its first six months in office. The government is currently balancing significant welfare gestures, such as the allocation of additional relief for the 2018 anti-Sterlite protest victims, with the necessity of maintaining internal party discipline following serious allegations of misconduct against its own legislators. This period is marked by a dual-track strategy: accelerating infrastructure and industrial growth through initiatives like electric vehicle experience centers, while simultaneously managing a robust opposition critique from the AIADMK regarding law and order.

Simultaneously, the state's judicial and administrative machinery is grappling with a sophisticated procurement scandal involving AI-generated books in public libraries, which has drawn sharp criticism from both the Congress and the DMK. The political friction is further intensified by legal actions against DMK members for defamatory remarks against the Chief Minister, signaling a tightening of social media and public discourse regulations. As the TVK government attempts to solidify its governance credentials, the interplay between judicial oversight on procurement and the executive's response to legislative indiscretion will define the state's political stability in the coming quarter.

This investigation highlights the administration's strict stance on political slander and the use of legal channels to address personal attacks on high-ranking officials. The case has sparked debate over the boundaries of political criticism versus defamation, as the DMK legislator faces scrutiny for remarks that the police claim crossed legal thresholds. ## Political Parties and Opposition
- **AIADMK Slams TVK Government on Law and Order:** AIADMK General Secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami (EPS) launched a scathing attack on the TVK government, claiming a total breakdown of law and order following a series of murders during the Deepavali festival. He further asserted that the TVK government has failed to fulfill its election promises and will provide no benefit to the public even if it completes its full five-year term. These remarks signal the AIADMK's strategy to position itself as the primary defender of public safety against the fledgling TVK administration. ## Assembly, Courts, and Policy
- **Madras High Court Orders FIR in AI-Generated Book Scam:** The Madras High Court has directed the registration of a First Information Report (FIR) and the freezing of bank accounts belonging to publishers involved in a scam where AI-generated books were sold to public libraries. The court flagged the presence of fake works attributed to authors like Jean Dreze and Bharathiyar, describing the incident as a serious breach of public trust. Simultaneously, DMK Legal Wing joint secretary I. Paranthamen filed a writ petition seeking a DVAC probe into a related ₹50-crore irregularity in book procurement for the Periyar Arivulagam library in Coimbatore. TNCC President Manickam Tagore has blamed the 2023 'transparent book procurement' policy introduced under the previous DMK regime for enabling these irregularities by allowing reader suggestions to bypass expert selection committees. The court's intervention marks a significant move to sanitize the state's library procurement system and hold officials accountable for digital-age fraud. ## District-Level Developments
- **High Court Demands DGP Response on Social Media Policing:** The Madras High Court has ordered the Tamil Nadu Director General of Police (DGP) to respond to a Public Interest Litigation (PIL) seeking guidelines to prevent the misuse of criminal laws against political social media posts. The petitioner, lawyer Syed Aleem, alleged that police across various districts are mechanically registering cases against political critics and activists without proper investigation. This development follows a surge in arrests related to online political commentary, which the petitioner claims stifles democratic dissent. The court's demand for a formal response from the DGP indicates a judicial intent to balance law enforcement needs with constitutional rights to free speech. The outcome could lead to a standardized protocol for all district police stations, potentially reducing the number of 'frivolous' FIRs filed against social media users for political views. ([News18 Tamil Nadu](https://tamil.news18.com/tamil-nadu/social-media-post-case-tamil-nadu-dgp-reply-high-court-nw-kpk-ws-l-2250690.html))
